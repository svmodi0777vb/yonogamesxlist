Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

YonoGamesXList (“we”, “this site”) is a static information directory. You do not need an account to browse. We do not process deposits, withdrawals or in-game balances.

1. Information we may collect

  • Server / hosting logs — IP address, browser type, pages viewed, date and time (standard for security and uptime).
  • Optional analytics — if we enable a basic analytics tool later, it may collect aggregated page views. We will not use it to sell personal profiles.
  • Email you send us — if you write to yonogamesxlist@gmail.com, we keep the message to reply and to fix listings.

We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank OTP, UPI PIN or wallet screenshots on this website.

2. Cookies

This site may use essential cookies needed for basic operation. Third-party referral or ad destinations can set their own cookies; their policies apply on those domains.

3. How we use information

  • To keep the site online and secure
  • To understand which pages are opened (if analytics is on)
  • To respond to contact emails about this directory

We do not sell personal information.

4. Third-party links

Download buttons and referral links leave yonogamesxlist.com. Those operators have their own privacy practices. Read their notices before you register or deposit.

5. Data retention

Server logs are kept only as long as the host normally retains them for security. Emails about listings may be kept until the issue is closed.

6. Your choices

You can browse without creating an account. You can clear cookies in your browser. For privacy questions about this directory only, email yonogamesxlist@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this page when our practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
